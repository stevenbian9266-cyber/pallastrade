First, you'd need to decide which PallasTrade edition is a good fit for your project:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Community Edition" href="https://github.com/stevenbian9266-cyber/pallastrade/">
    PallasTrade Community Edition is free. It's the DIY version of PallasTrade. It comes with community support only.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enterprise Edition" href="https://pallastrade.cn/enterprise/">
    PallasTrade Enterprise Edition comes with more features, more security, more integrations and enterprise support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

So what sets both versions apart? Here's a [feature comparison](https://pallastrade.cn/enterprise/) of the two editions and here's an [in-depth overview](https://pallastrade.cn/pallastrade-commerce-version-comparison-community-edition-vs-enterprise-edition/).

To start using PallasTrade, either version, the steps are usually:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Setup">
    Set up your PallasTrade application (backend and storefront)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize">
    Customize PallasTrade to meet your business needs
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live!">
    Deploy to your preferred hosting/cloud provider
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Need implementation guidance? Start with the [developer documentation](https://pallastrade.cn/docs/developer) or open an issue.</Tip>

## Looking for Developer Documentation?
