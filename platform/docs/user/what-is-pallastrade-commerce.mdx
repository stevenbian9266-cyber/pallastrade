## About PallasTrade Commerce

[PallasTrade Commerce](https://pallastrade.cn/) is an API-first, [open-source eCommerce platform](https://github.com/stevenbian9266-cyber/pallastrade) built for fast growing businesses and enterprises.

PallasTrade allows you to build [B2B eCommerce](https://pallastrade.cn/use-cases/headless-b2b-ecommerce/), [multi-store](https://pallastrade.cn/multi-store-ecommerce/), [multi-region](https://pallastrade.cn/multi-region-ecommerce/), [multi-vendor marketplace](https://pallastrade.cn/marketplace-ecommerce/), and [multi-tenant commerce](https://pallastrade.cn/multi-tenant-white-label-ecommerce/) - and you can combine them to match your exact business model.

If SaaS solutions don't give you the control and flexibility your business needs, PallasTrade is the answer.

PallasTrade is trusted by thousands of businesses around the world, including GoDaddy, Huckberry, Craftsman, DieHard, Kenmore, Paccar, Maisonette, Goop, Garmentory, and Blue Bottle Coffee.

PallasTrade comes in two flavors - as a free Community Edition or as a paid Enterprise Edition.

  <Card title="Community Edition" href="https://github.com/stevenbian9266-cyber/pallastrade/">
    PallasTrade Community Edition is free. It's the DIY version of PallasTrade. It comes with community support only.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enterprise Edition" href="https://pallastrade.cn/enterprise/">

  </Card>

TL;DR The Community Edition gives you freedom. The Enterprise Edition saves you time.

So what sets both versions apart? Here's a [feature comparison](https://pallastrade.cn/enterprise/) of the two editions and here's an [in-depth overview](https://pallastrade.cn/pallastrade-commerce-version-comparison-community-edition-vs-enterprise-edition/).
