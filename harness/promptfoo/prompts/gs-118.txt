# Eval Scenario GS-118: Canonical cart store credit is intent-only, mutually exclusive with gift cards, and legacy cart traffic stays measurable

PRD-20260914-checkout-cart-store-credits-canonical (legacy convergence slice 2): POST/DELETE /api/v3/store/carts/:cart_id/store_credits answered 404 cart_not_found for cart_ ids, so a logged-in shopper could never use their balance; the endpoint now resolves canonical carts, records cart-stage intent, enforces the order-level gift-card exclusivity up front, and the remaining order-domain legacy endpoints report usage so migration is traffic-driven

## MUST do
- Dual-resolve the cart id: cart_ -> current_store.shopping_carts, anything else -> the legacy CartResolvable lookup with a cart.legacy_flow.used observation entry
- Keep authentication mandatory (store credit is account property) and validate credits exist in the cart currency before storing the intent — omitting amount stores the full available total
- Redeem at submit through the authoritative Checkout::AddStoreCredit (after the amount pipeline so outstanding_balance is final), creating the PaymentMethod::StoreCredit on demand, and fail the submission with no Order when the credit is gone
- Persist any change when ensuring the store-credit payment method (an existing-but-disabled record must be saved active, otherwise the available scope stays empty and the authoritative service raises), and convert that raise into an order-less submission failure instead of a 500
- Enforce gift-card/store-credit mutual exclusivity at intent time in both directions, mirroring GiftCards::Apply gift_card_using_store_credit_error
- Emit cart.legacy_flow.used (one flow_type per endpoint) for every remaining legacy cart endpoint — payments, fulfillments, gift cards, store credits, discount codes — instead of guessing which ones to migrate; cart_ requests are never counted as legacy traffic

## MUST NOT do
- Create a payment or move store credit balance at cart stage (pallastrade_carts has no payments)
- Let the authoritative service raise out of the submit (missing PaymentMethod::StoreCredit) instead of preparing it, turning a shop configuration gap into a 500
- Accept a gift card intent and a store credit intent on the same cart, or count cart_ requests as legacy traffic
- Save the payment method only when it is a new record (silent no-op on an existing disabled row) or let a provider/service exception escape as a 500
