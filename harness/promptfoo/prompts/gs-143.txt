# Eval Scenario GS-143: Reconciliation differences become a queue a person can work

PRD-20260916-payments-d13-reconciliation-cases (slice 1 of 搂78-D13 / 搂70.1 of the payment plan): the P4 reconcilers are read-only and deliberately do not persist anything, so mismatches only reached a log line and a rake command. D13 slice 1 persists them as reconciliation cases with an assignee, notes and a close/reopen lifecycle, syncs them from the hourly sweeper, and exposes a back-office workbench with CSV export.

## MUST do
- Keep the queue a work queue: syncing and every case action write only case rows and audit entries, never payments, refunds, transactions, the ledger, orders or stock
- Feed the queue from the existing read-only reconcilers instead of writing a second reconciliation implementation, and keep the journal-repair branch of the sweeper untouched
- Key every case on a deterministic signature of the reason codes so repeated sweeps touch the same case (occurrences/last_seen_at) instead of piling up duplicates
- Close cases automatically when the difference disappears or is superseded, but never overwrite a decision a person already made (explained, resolved, dismissed)
- Expose difference type and severity from one deterministic mapping shared by the model, the page and the CSV
- Require a reason to dismiss, require a body for a note, keep the notes append-only, and write an audit entry for every action
- Export exactly what the current filters show, with a row cap and a warning when the cap truncates the file

## MUST NOT do
- Move money or change payment/refund/order state from the queue, or call the provider from a case action
- Auto-reopen a case a human dismissed, or auto-close one that a human is still investigating without recording why
- Let the CSV and the page disagree about filters, or export unbounded result sets
- Rename the transaction association to something that clashes with ActiveRecord, or expose raw integer ids in the UI
- Forget the navigation registration/consistency test or the permission check for the new page
