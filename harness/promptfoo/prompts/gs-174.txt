# Eval Scenario GS-174: A stuck payment that only a human may unstick

PRD-20260917-payments-d2 (business plan §78-D2 / §60.2-3): a transaction stopped for manual review had no way out except editing its state in a console, because the automatic recovery loop is deliberately forbidden for it. The operator now gets exactly two verdicts - approve & capture, or reject & release - each reusing existing capture/finalize/cancel primitives, each refusing when the facts do not support it, and each leaving an audit trail that says who decided what, when, and why.

## MUST do
- Keep the verdicts human-only: no job, sweeper or subscriber may capture or release on the operator's behalf, and other states keep using automatic recovery
- Approve & capture: require a pending authorization, capture it through the provider, then reuse the existing finalize path so participants complete and inventory commits
- Reject & release: never capture, void the pending authorization, release inventory through the existing cancellation primitive and cancel the transaction with zero refunds
- Refuse rather than guess, without changing state: no pending authorization blocks capture, an already captured payment blocks release
- Require a reason on both verdicts and record decision, reason, actor and before/after state in the audit trail, including refusals
- Make a repeated verdict with the same decision a no-op replay that reports already_applied instead of moving money twice
- Keep the operator's free-text reason out of the cancel-reason enum: map it to the canonical staff reason and keep the text in the note and the audit
- Leave the existing recovery edges (reopen_review / retry / repair) untouched and prove it with the transaction regressions

## MUST NOT do
- Let any automated path call the review service, or let a replayed verdict move money twice
- Capture without a pending authorization, or cancel a transaction whose money was already captured while pretending nothing was paid
- Create a refund from the release verdict, rewrite historical payment/refund/ledger rows, or leave a voidable authorization alive after releasing
- Half-apply a verdict (state changed while a step failed) or require the operator to edit the transaction from a console
- Show verdict buttons for states that are not manual review, or ship the actions without localization and audit rendering

- capturePath: 20
- releasePath: 20
