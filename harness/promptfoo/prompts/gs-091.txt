# Eval Scenario GS-091: Dispute ingestion (durable dispute + provider event entry)

Provider-initiated money reversals must become durable facts without touching order/inventory/finance semantics, reusing the P0 webhook inbox (see PRD-20260911-payments-dsp-p7-1)

## MUST do
- Subscribe and map charge.dispute.* events in the gateway, routing them to a dispute handler that does NOT require a payment_session (payment/checkout paths unchanged)
- Persist a durable PallasTrade::Dispute keyed by (provider, provider_dispute_reference) with idempotent upsert (nil never overwrites existing facts)
- Keep the state machine forward-only by phase rank (jumps allowed, rollbacks rejected) and record evidence_due_at as a first-class column
- Keep events durable when no local payment matches (attention_reason=unlinked_payment) instead of dropping or failing the webhook
- Reuse PaymentWebhookEvent dedupe/replay/retry and keep zero business side effects (no order cancel, no stock movement, no journal posting)

## MUST NOT do
- Route dispute events through the payment webhook service or cancel/restock orders from a webhook
- Reuse REFUND_SUCCEEDED / Refund semantics for chargebacks, or mutate CommerceTransaction state
- Assume 1 payment = 1 dispute or full-amount disputes only
- Lose a dispute event because the provider payload has no resolvable local anchor
