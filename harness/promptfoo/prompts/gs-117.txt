# Eval Scenario GS-117: Canonical cart gift cards resolve on cart_ ids and stay money-free until submit

PRD-20260914-checkout-cart-gift-cards-canonical (legacy convergence slice 1): POST/DELETE /api/v3/store/carts/:cart_id/gift_cards only worked on legacy order-backed carts, so every cart_ call answered 404 cart_not_found while the storefront BFF was already calling it; the endpoint now dual-resolves and records cart-stage intent, redeeming the card when the cart is submitted

## MUST do
- Dual-resolve the cart id: cart_ -> current_store.shopping_carts (guest/user scoped, active only), anything else -> the legacy CartResolvable lookup with a [legacy-gift-cards] observation log
- Keep cart stage money-free: validate the code (gift_card_not_found / gift_card_expired / gift_card_already_redeemed) and persist cart.private_metadata['gift_card_code'] without creating a payment or reserving balance
- Redeem at submit through the existing order.apply_gift_card authority, and fail the submission (no Order) when the stored card is no longer usable instead of silently charging full price
- Apply the card only after the amount pipeline has persisted the final order.total (then re-run update_with_updater! so payment_total/amount_due refresh), and skip redemption entirely on a zero-total order
- Keep the error contract identical to the legacy endpoint (404 gift_card_not_found, 422 expired/already redeemed) so one BFF mapping serves both cart kinds
- Expose only code + display_amount_remaining on the shopping-cart serializer, and keep removal idempotent

## MUST NOT do
- Create a store-credit payment or consume gift card balance at cart stage (pallastrade_carts has no payments)
- Duplicate gift card money logic outside order.apply_gift_card / gift_card_apply_service
- Change legacy cart behaviour, or drop the [legacy-gift-cards] log that tracks remaining legacy traffic
- Compute the redemption amount from a stale order.total (applying before the updater made the store credit zero and failed the submission), or write money logic that only asserts 'amount_used > 0' instead of the amount relationship
