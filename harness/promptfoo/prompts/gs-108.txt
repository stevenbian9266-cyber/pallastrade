# Eval Scenario GS-108: Dispute deadline alerts remind and escalate without automating anything

DSP-P7-10 B2 (FR-007): a subscriber turns the sweeper's due-soon / overdue events into audit trail, metrics and an operator-facing escalation flag, while the scanner itself stays read-only

## MUST do
- Keep the sweeper read-only and put notification/escalation in a subscriber that consumes its published events
- Leave an audit entry and a metric for due-soon alerts without changing any dispute field
- Escalate an overdue dispute by flagging attention only when no more specific reason is already set
- Skip terminal disputes and unknown ids quietly, and never let a failure bubble into the sweeper
- Register the subscriber in the engine list and assert that registration in a spec
- Support both prefixed and raw ids in the event payload

## MUST NOT do
- Automate submission, acceptance, refunds, restock or re-billing from a reminder event
- Overwrite a more specific attention reason, or advance the dispute state machine
- Send a reminder and leave it untraceable (no audit, no metric)

## Scoring
- scannerStaysReadOnly: 25
- dueSoonTraceOnly: 20
- overdueEscalation: 25
- noAutomation: 20
- registrationAsserted: 10
