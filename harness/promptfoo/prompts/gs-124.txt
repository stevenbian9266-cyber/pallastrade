# Eval Scenario GS-124: Legacy cart routes stay served, become machine-readably deprecated, and never gain new consumers

PRD-20260915-checkout B5: the six legacy cart-domain routes of the §45 migration matrix must keep serving existing traffic unchanged, carry a machine-readable deprecation signal only for legacy identity (never for canonical cart_ traffic), log one unified usage-metric contract that preserves the historical keys, and stay free of new consumers until the 30-day zero-traffic retirement threshold is met

## MUST do
- Emit Deprecation/Warning/Link successor headers on legacy-identity (non cart_) requests only, keeping status codes, bodies and side effects unchanged
- Log one unified metric per request with flow_type, entry_point, requested_cart_id, legacy_identity, action, deprecated and canonical_successor, preserving the historical payment.legacy_flow.used key and legacy markers
- Mark the routes as deprecated in the OpenAPI contract (backend copy plus platform mirror) and in the SDK method docs, pointing at the canonical successor each time
- Document the retirement threshold (30 consecutive days of zero legacy traffic before an independent removal project) and the ban on adding new capability to legacy routes
- Keep the storefront/mobile consumer guard green: no cart-domain payment sessions, payments or cart-complete calls, and shared cart routes confined to the canonical cart_ whitelist

## MUST NOT do
- Deprecate-mark canonical cart_ traffic that shares the same route shape, or count it as legacy traffic
- Change or drop the historical metric keys/markers that existing log pipelines already consume
- Delete legacy controllers or routes to 'clean up' before the traffic threshold is met, or add new features to a legacy route

## Scoring
- legacyIdentityOnlySignal: 30
- unifiedMetricContract: 25
- contractAndSdkDeprecation: 20
- retirementPolicy: 15
- zeroNewConsumers: 10
